Tab 2: Backtesting Engine — Would You Fly an Untested Plane?

The second tab of the Smart Trading Center is the Historical Backtesting Engine. It takes the exact same signal strategies the scanner uses in real-time and replays them against historical market data — giving you a scientific answer to the question: "How would this strategy have performed in the past?"

The most expensive mistake a trader can make is deploying real capital on a strategy they've never tested. Backtesting costs you nothing. Skipping it could cost you everything.

📋 Step-by-Step: How to Run a Backtest

1
Load Market Data First

Go back to the main scanner and click 🔄 Refresh Data. The backtester uses this live data as the starting point for its simulation. Without data, the engine cannot run.

2
Choose a Preset Scenario (or go Custom)

Select from the dropdown — Swing Trade, Quick Scalp, Trend Hunter, or Conservative Safe. The fields populate automatically. Or set your own Capital, Trade Size, Stop Loss, Take Profit, Strategy, and Market Trend.

3
Optionally Enable Trailing Stop

Check the Trailing Stop checkbox. This moves the stop loss upward as the trade profits, locking in gains. Most effective in trending markets — test with and without it.

4
Click 🚀 Run Backtest

The engine runs in a background thread — the UI stays fully responsive. Each simulated trade appears in the left table. The right console shows win rate, net P&L, and key statistics in real-time.

5
Analyze & Iterate

Look for strategies with a win rate above 50% and a positive expectancy. Change one variable at a time. The goal is to find parameters that are consistently profitable across different market conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Paper Trading and Backtesting?
Paper Trading (Tab 1) runs forward in time using live market data. It opens virtual trades when signals fire right now and tracks them in real-time. Backtesting (Tab 2) runs backward in time, replaying your strategy against historical data. Use backtesting to validate a strategy, then use paper trading to confirm it works in current market conditions before going live with real money.
How do I change the TP and SL for Paper Trading?
In the Smart Trading Center (📊 button in the main toolbar), go to Tab 1: Paper Trading. At the top, you will see the Paper Trade Settings bar with three fields: Trade Size ($), Take Profit (%), and Stop Loss (%). Change the values and click Apply PnL to instantly recalculate all current open trades under the new parameters.
How many paper trades should I run before going live?
A minimum of 30 completed trades is recommended for statistical significance. Below 20, win rate estimates are unreliable noise. Above 50 gives you a very solid picture of whether your chosen TP/SL scenario is profitable with the current signal strategy.
What does the AI Trade Learner actually block?
The AI blocks (or flags with a warning) signals that match patterns from your personal loss history. For example, if you have lost money 3 times in a row when RSI > 72 during a BTC correction, the system will automatically block future signals in that exact condition. Rules are personalized to your trade history — not generic advice. All rules are visible and editable in the Learning Dashboard.
Does the backtester use real historical data?
When the full backtesting engine is installed (backtesting/backtest.py), it uses real OHLCV data fetched from your configured API source. If the engine module is missing, the simulator runs a Monte Carlo simulation based on your TP/SL parameters and a random seed — useful for understanding R/R math, but not for strategy validation. Premium users get full live-data backtesting.
Can I use the bot in simulator mode before connecting to a real exchange?
Yes. Set the exchange to SIMULATOR in the bot settings.
