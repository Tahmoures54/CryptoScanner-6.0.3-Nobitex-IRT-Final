CryptoScanner — Real Trading Data Flow

REAL AUTO TRADING — OBSERVED COIN MOVEMENT
1. Nobitex /market/stats (IRT/Rial markets) -> live local universe and books.
2. CoinMarketCap listings + quotes for overlapping symbols -> USD prices this cycle.
3. Engine stores CMC USD and Nobitex ask every scan and measures lookback movement.
4. GlobalLeadEngine keeps a candidate when the coin is moving (CMC 1h or observed USD path)
   and quality filters pass (spread, volume, freshness, BTC dump, local already done).
5. It does not require Nobitex to be cheaper than CMC × USDT/IRT.
6. Confirmed candidates go to the live SignalTracker and are shadowed on Paper Trading.
7. Real SignalTracker -> TradingBot -> Nobitex order API.
8. Fill confirmation -> native stop_market -> trailing -> exit.

IMPORTANT
- CoinMarketCap is intelligence only; it never receives orders.
- Nobitex is the sole execution venue.
- IRT balances and size are Rial, matching the Nobitex wallet.
- Trading size, stops, and Global Lead live in SmartEagle Bot → Settings.
- Scanner Settings is market-data API / display only.
