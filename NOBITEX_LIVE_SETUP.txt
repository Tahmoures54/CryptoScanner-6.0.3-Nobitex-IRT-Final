CRYPTO SCANNER 6.0.3 — NOBITEX LIVE AUTO TRADING
================================================

This build is optimized for Nobitex IRT spot markets.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. Live auto-scan uses Nobitex /market/stats directly.
2. The live SignalTracker is now connected to TradingBot/Nobitex.
3. Paper Trading remains separate in its own database.
4. Real positions use real Nobitex fills, not simulated fills.
5. After a real BUY fill, a native Nobitex stop_market SELL is placed.
6. Trailing stops replace the exchange-side stop safely (new stop first, old stop second).
7. Low-priced assets keep sufficient price precision.
8. Market orders use a small slippage guard based on the current Nobitex ask.
9. The complete Nobitex market snapshot is processed every 30 seconds so open positions
   continue to be monitored even when there is no new entry signal.
10. Exposure, max positions, cooldowns and drawdown protections remain active.

DEFAULT REAL SETTINGS
---------------------
Exchange: Nobitex
Quote: IRT
Spot: Yes
Auto trading: enabled when the configured Nobitex executor is READY
Max open positions: 3
Max exposure: 50% of current equity
Position target: 5,000,000 IRT
Minimum order: 3,000,000 IRT
Initial stop: 3%
Trailing activation: 1.5%
Trailing distance: 1.5%
Scan interval: 30 seconds
New entries per cycle: 1
Confirmation: enabled

IMPORTANT
---------
No trading strategy can guarantee profit or pay for a trip. The purpose of this build is
reliable execution and risk containment, not guaranteed returns.

Keep the API key restricted to trading only. Do not enable withdrawals for the bot key.
Start with a small allocation and verify one real order + one protective stop before
leaving the computer unattended.

The distribution ZIP intentionally does NOT include private credentials from the
original secrets/ directory.
